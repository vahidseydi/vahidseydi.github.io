                <p style="color:black; text-align:justify">I am a Senior Lecturer in Data Science/Data Analytics at UWE Bristol and an Honorary Research Fellow at Bangor University's School of Ocean Sciences. From mathematics and software engineering to artificial intelligence, I have more than 20 years of experience, and my work, grounded in the mathematical foundations of machine learning, has followed the field's own development: from neural networks, fuzzy systems and evolutionary optimisation, through probabilistic models, reinforcement learning and transfer learning, to deep learning, transformers, large language models and diffusion models. My approach centres on formulating problems precisely, understanding their underlying structure, developing appropriate solutions, and establishing why they work and under what conditions, a question that runs from the interpretable rules of fuzzy systems to today's explainable AI. I apply this approach across domains; as a Research Fellow at Bangor (2020–2025), I used it in passive acoustic monitoring, marine mammal behaviour, wind farm site selection and an offshore wind digital twin. Before that I was Assistant Professor and Head of the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Azad University, South Tehran Branch. In 2023 I received a Global Talent Endorsement from the Royal Society.
